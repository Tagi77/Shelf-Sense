# Food catalogue notes

The UI footer/source block was removed on request. Reference metadata stays here for maintenance.

Whole/cut/ripeness storage suggestions are conservative freshness planning ranges for newly purchased fresh food. They cannot determine its age, safety or time already spent unrefrigerated. Packaging instructions and actual storage conditions take priority. A smell or photo cannot certify safety. Unknown foods do not receive a fabricated local expiry.

References consulted:
- https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/storing-garden-fruits-and-vegetables (berries, greens, roots, brassicas, peppers, dry pantry vegetables; whole vs cut)
- https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-guide/eating-support/kitchen/cooking-skills/storing-vegetables-fruits.html (ripening and citrus)
- https://extension.k-state.edu/foodsafety/produce/resources/docs/storage-for-better-taste.pdf (cold-sensitive produce, room storage)
- https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/foodkeeper-app
- https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts
- https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/safe-minimum-internal-temperatures

Actual Unsplash image URLs are retained in `public/photo-credits.json`; 12 more produce photographs were downloaded and visually checked. Generated images, when enabled later, are illustrations of a food name and never evidence of the user's product condition.
